        Squatlegs8 to 12 reps
        How
        1. Feet a little wider than your hips, toes turned out slightly.
        2. Sit back and down for a slow count of three, knees tracking over your toes.
        3. Go as low as you can with heels flat and chest up.
        4. Push the floor away to stand. Breathe out on the way up.
        5. To add weight, hold one dumbbell upright against your chest.
        Feel it here
        • Front of the thighs, most of all near the bottom.
        • Glutes as you stand up.
        • Trunk braced and tight, not strained.
        Not this
        • Low back doing the work: your chest dropped or your hips shot up first.
        • Weight on your toes or heels lifting.
        • Pain inside the knee joint. Muscle burn around it is fine.
        Split squatlegs6 to 10 reps each leg
        How
        1. Take a long step forward and stay there. Back heel is off the floor.
        2. Lower straight down until the back knee is just above the floor.
        3. Push through the whole front foot to come up.
        4. Finish all reps on one side, then switch. Hold a wall if balance is the hard part.
        Feel it here
        • Thigh and glute of the front leg.
        • A stretch at the front of the back hip.
        Not this
        • Front heel lifting: step out longer.
        • Front knee caving inward.
        • Pushing mostly off the back foot.
        Glute bridgehips10 to 15 reps, 2-second hold at the top
        How
        1. Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat and close enough to brush your heels with your fingertips.
        2. Flatten your low back gently into the floor.
        3. Push through your heels and lift your hips until you're a straight ramp.
        4. Squeeze for two seconds, then lower slowly.
        Feel it here
        • Glutes, squeezing hard at the top.
        • A little in the backs of the thighs.
        Not this
        • Low back pinching: you lifted too high. Stop at the ramp.
        • Hamstrings cramping: bring your feet closer to your hips.
        Hip hingehips8 to 12 reps
        How
        1. Stand tall holding the weights in front of your thighs, knees soft.
        2. Push your hips straight back and let the weights slide down your legs.
        3. Stop when you feel a strong stretch behind the thighs, usually around the knees or mid-shin.
        4. Squeeze your glutes to stand back up.
        5. No weights: do the same with your hands sliding down your thighs.
        Feel it here
        • A stretch, then work, in the backs of the thighs.
        • Glutes as you stand.
        Not this
        • Back rounding: you went lower than your hamstrings allow.
        • Knees bending a lot. That turns it into a squat.
        • Weights drifting away from your legs.
        Dead bugcore6 to 10 slow reps each side
        How
        1. On your back, arms straight up, knees bent above your hips.
        2. Press your low back into the floor and keep it there.
        3. Slowly lower one arm overhead and the opposite leg toward the floor.
        4. Stop just before your back wants to lift, return, switch sides.
        Feel it here
        • Deep abs, like bracing before a cough.
        • It gets harder the further the leg reaches.
        Not this
        • Low back arching off the floor: shorten the reach.
        • Holding your breath. Breathe out as you reach.
        • Neck straining: rest your head on the floor.
        Plankcore20 to 60 seconds
        How
        1. Forearms on the floor, elbows under shoulders, legs straight back on your toes.
        2. Squeeze your glutes and pull your belly button gently toward your spine.
        3. Breathe normally and hold. Stop when the line breaks, not when the timer says.
        4. Easier: knees on the floor.
        Feel it here
        • Abs, all the way across the front.
        • Glutes and thighs helping.
        Not this
        • Low back aching: your hips sagged. Reset or stop.
        • Hips piked high to make it easier.
        Side plankcore15 to 40 seconds each side
        How
        1. Lie on your side, forearm on the floor, elbow under your shoulder, feet stacked.
        2. Lift your hips until ankles, hips and shoulders line up.
        3. Hold and breathe. Switch sides.
        4. Easier: bend your knees and hold from the knees.
        Feel it here
        • The side of your waist closest to the floor.
        • The outside of the lower hip.
        Not this
        • Hips dropping toward the floor.
        • Shoulder pain in the support arm: check the elbow is under the shoulder.
        Push-uppush5 to 12 reps
        How
        1. Hands under your shoulders, a little wider. Body straight from heels to head.
        2. Lower your chest for a count of two, elbows angled back.
        3. Push the floor away until your arms are straight.
        4. Easier: put your hands on a kitchen counter or the arm of a sofa. Lower the surface as it gets easy.
        Feel it here
        • Chest and the backs of your arms.
        • Abs holding you straight.
        Not this
        • Hips sagging or head reaching for the floor first.
        • Elbows flared straight out, which pinches the shoulder.
        Overhead presspush8 to 12 reps
        How
        1. Stand with dumbbells at your shoulders, elbows slightly in front of you.
        2. Squeeze your glutes and keep your ribs down.
        3. Press straight up until your arms are straight beside your ears.
        4. Lower for a count of two.
        Feel it here
        • Shoulders, then the backs of your arms near the top.
        Not this
        • Leaning back and arching: the weight is too heavy.
        • A pinch at the top of the shoulder. Stop and go lighter.
        Dumbbell bench presspush8 to 12 reps
        How
        1. Lie on the bench, feet flat, dumbbells held above your chest.
        2. Lower them for a count of two until they're level with your chest, elbows angled about 45 degrees from your sides.
        3. Press back up until your arms are straight.
        4. No bench: do it lying on the floor. Your elbows stop at the floor, which is fine.
        Feel it here
        • Chest, with a stretch at the bottom.
        • Backs of the arms as you finish.
        Not this
        • Shoulders rolling forward off the bench.
        • Weights wobbling: go lighter until they travel straight.
        Lateral raisepush10 to 15 reps
        How
        1. Stand with a light dumbbell in each hand, elbows slightly bent.
        2. Raise your arms out to the sides until they're level with your shoulders.
        3. Pause, then lower for a count of three.
        4. No light weights: use water bottles or tins.
        Feel it here
        • The outside cap of the shoulder. It burns quickly; that's the point.
        Not this
        • Swinging or leaning back to get the weight up. It's too heavy.
        • Shrugging your shoulders toward your ears.
        Supported rowpull8 to 12 reps each arm
        How
        1. One hand on a bench, chair or counter, back flat, dumbbell hanging from the other hand.
        2. Pull your elbow back toward your hip until the weight reaches your ribs.
        3. Pause for a beat, then lower for a slow count of two.
        4. No dumbbell: a backpack loaded with books works the same way.
        Feel it here
        • Between the shoulder blade and spine, and down the side of your back.
        • Some biceps is normal.
        Not this
        • Shoulder shrugging toward your ear.
        • Torso twisting open to heave the weight up: go lighter.
        • Low back aching: lean more weight into the support hand.
        Dead hangpull10 to 30 seconds
        How
        1. Grip the bar a little wider than your shoulders, palms facing away.
        2. Lift your feet and hang with straight arms.
        3. Draw your shoulders gently down, away from your ears, and breathe.
        4. Easier: keep your toes on a chair and take only part of your weight.
        Feel it here
        • Forearms and fingers working to hold on.
        • A long stretch down your sides.
        Not this
        • Sharp pain in the shoulder or elbow. Come down.
        • Hanging to total failure. Let go while your grip is still sure.
        Band pull-apartpull12 to 20 reps
        How
        1. Hold a band in front of your chest, arms straight, hands shoulder-width apart.
        2. Pull your hands apart until the band touches your chest.
        3. Squeeze your shoulder blades together for a beat.
        4. Return slowly, keeping some tension in the band.
        Feel it here
        • Between your shoulder blades and across the back of the shoulders.
        Not this
        • Shoulders shrugging up.
        • Ribs flaring and back arching to finish the rep.
        Soft landingjump3 sets of 5, done first
        How
        1. Reach tall on your toes, arms overhead.
        2. Drop fast: throw your arms down and back and pull your feet off the floor a little.
        3. Land on the whole foot in a quarter squat and freeze for two seconds.
        Feel it here
        • Thighs and glutes catching you.
        • Hips sitting back as the knees bend.
        Not this
        • A jolt in the knees or shins.
        • Knees caving toward each other.
        • Reps getting slower or louder: the set is over.
        Calf raisejump10 to 20 reps
        How
        1. Stand with fingertips on a wall for balance.
        2. Rise onto the balls of your feet as high as you can.
        3. Pause for a second, then lower for a count of three.
        4. Harder: one leg at a time, or heels hanging off a stair.
        Feel it here
        • The calf muscle, high on the back of the lower leg.
        Not this
        • Ankles rolling outward at the top. Press through the big toe.
        • Bouncing. The slow lowering is the useful part.
        Box jumpjump3 to 5 jumps, full rest between sets
        How
        1. Stand a short step from a sturdy low box or stair.
        2. Dip, swing your arms and jump, landing softly with your whole foot on the box.
        3. Stand up fully, then step down one foot at a time.
        4. Rest until you feel fresh. These are for height, not for sweat.
        Feel it here
        • A quick, springy push from calves, thighs and glutes.
        Not this
        • Landing in a deep tucked squat: the box is too high.
        • Jumps getting lower. Stop the set.
        • Doing these on a volleyball day.
        Hip-flexor stretchmobility30 seconds each side
        How
        1. Kneel on one knee, other foot flat in front. Put a towel under the knee.
        2. Stay tall. Tuck your tailbone under, as if zipping up tight jeans.
        3. Squeeze the glute on the kneeling side and ease your hips forward a few centimetres.
        4. Hold and breathe slowly.
        Feel it here
        • A firm pull at the front of the hip on the kneeling leg, maybe down the thigh.
        Not this

        Stand stackedmobility10 seconds, whenever you notice
        How
        1. Shift your weight to the middle of your feet, off your heels.
        2. Grow tall through the crown of your head.
        3. Breathe out slowly and let your ribs settle over your hips.
        Feel it here
        • Length through the front of your trunk.
        • Light work in the glutes and low abs.
        Not this
        • Chest pushed up and low back arched. That's "shoulders back" overdone.
        • A rigid hold. Ten seconds, then move on.
        Knee-to-wall checkmobilityonce now, again each month
        How
        1. Face a wall, one foot forward, toes a hand's width from it.
        2. Drive that knee straight over your toes to touch the wall, heel flat.
        3. Move the foot back bit by bit until the knee only just reaches.
        4. Measure toe to wall. Repeat on the other side.
        Feel it here
        • A stretch low in the calf and behind the ankle.
        What to look for
        • A clear gap between left and right is the useful finding. Stiff ankles change how you land.
        • A pinch at the front of the ankle is worth mentioning to a physical therapist.
        Incline walk intervalscardio4 to 6 rounds: 2 to 3 minutes brisk, 1 minute easy
        How
        1. Set a moderate incline and walk easily for three minutes.
        2. Speed up to a brisk walk for two to three minutes.
        3. Slow to an easy walk for one minute. That's one round.
        4. No treadmill: a hill or a few flights of stairs does the same job.
        Feel it here
        • Breathing clearly harder, but you can still speak.
        • Calves and glutes working on the slope.
        Not this
        • Hanging onto the rails, which takes the work away.
        • Gasping. Lower the speed or the incline.
        Goblet squatlegs8 to 12 reps
        How
        1. Hold one dumbbell upright by its top end, against your chest, elbows pointing down.
        2. Feet a little wider than your hips, toes turned out slightly.
        3. Sit down between your heels, elbows brushing the inside of your knees.
        4. Go as low as you can with your chest up and heels down, then stand by pushing the floor away.
        Feel it here
        • Front of the thighs.
        • Glutes, more the deeper you go.
        • Upper back, keeping the weight close.
        Not this
        • Heels lifting: widen your stance a little, or stay higher for now.
        • Knees caving in: push them out over your toes.
        • The weight drifting away from your chest: go lighter.
        Reverse lungelegs6 to 10 reps each leg
        How
        1. Stand tall, feet hip-width apart.
        2. Step one foot back about two feet and land on the ball of that foot.
        3. Lower until the back knee nearly touches the floor, front shin close to upright.
        4. Push through the front heel to step back up. Finish one side, then the other.
        Feel it here
        • Front thigh and glute of the front leg.
        • A stretch at the front of the back hip.
        Not this
        • Front knee pain: take a longer step back.
        • Wobbling: hold a wall or a chair with one hand.
        • The back knee banging down: slow the last few inches.
        Step-uplegs8 to 12 reps each leg
        How
        1. Stand facing a sturdy step or box, knee height or lower.
        2. Put your whole foot on it.
        3. Lean forward slightly and drive through that heel to stand up on top.
        4. Step down slowly with the other foot. Do all the reps on one leg, then switch.
        Feel it here
        • Front thigh and glute of the leg on the box.
        Not this
        • Pushing off the floor with the back foot: the top leg does the lifting.
        • The box wobbling: use something that cannot slide or tip.
        • Knee pain: use a lower step.
        Hip thrusthips10 to 15 reps, 2-second hold at the top
        How
        1. Sit on the floor with your upper back, just below the shoulder blades, against the long side of a bench that will not slide.
        2. Knees bent, feet flat and about hip-width apart.
        3. Tuck your chin and push through your heels until your thighs and torso are level.
        4. Squeeze for two seconds, then lower under control.
        Feel it here
        • Glutes, hard at the top.
        • Some hamstrings.
        Not this
        • Low back arching at the top: stop when you are level and keep your ribs down.
        • The bench sliding: push it against a wall.
        • Shins slanting a lot at the top: move your feet until they are roughly upright.
        Bird dogcore6 to 10 slow reps each side
        How
        1. Start on hands and knees, hands under your shoulders, knees under your hips.
        2. Brace your stomach lightly, as if someone were about to poke it.
        3. Reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back until both are level with your body.
        4. Hold for a few seconds, come back, and switch sides.
        Feel it here
        • Low back and glutes, working to keep you still.
        • Stomach holding steady.
        Not this
        • The back arching or the hips twisting: do not lift the leg as high.
        • Rushing: a glass of water on your low back should not spill.
        Incline push-uppush6 to 15 reps
        How
        1. Put your hands on a sturdy counter, table or bench edge, a little wider than your shoulders.
        2. Walk your feet back until your body is one straight line.
        3. Lower your chest to the edge, elbows angled back about 45 degrees.
        4. Push the edge away until your arms are straight. When it gets easy, use a lower surface.
        Feel it here
        • Chest and the backs of the arms.
        • Core holding you straight.
        Not this
        • Hips sagging: squeeze your glutes.
        • Elbows flared straight out: tuck them toward your ribs.
        • The surface sliding: use something fixed, or push it against a wall.
        Reverse flypull10 to 15 reps
        How
        1. Hold a light dumbbell in each hand.
        2. Hinge at the hips until your torso is close to level, back flat, knees soft.
        3. Let the weights hang under your shoulders, palms facing each other.
        4. Keep a slight bend in the elbows and open your arms out to the sides until they are level with your back. Lower slowly.
        Feel it here
        • Backs of the shoulders.
        • Between the shoulder blades.
        Not this
        • Swinging your torso up to lift: go lighter.
        • Shrugging toward your ears: keep your shoulders down.
        • The low back tiring: rest your forehead on a bench, or sit and lean over your knees.
        Prone Y raisepull8 to 12 reps, 2-second hold
        How
        1. Lie face down with your forehead on a folded towel.
        2. Reach your arms overhead in a Y, thumbs pointing to the ceiling.
        3. Draw your shoulder blades down and back, then lift your arms a few inches off the floor.
        4. Hold for two seconds and lower.
        Feel it here
        • Between and below the shoulder blades.
        Not this
        • Lifting your chest to get the arms higher: only the arms rise.
        • Neck straining: keep your forehead on the towel.
        • Pinching at the front of the shoulder: bring your arms lower, closer to a T.
        Farmer carrypull20 to 40 seconds of walking
        How
        1. Pick up a dumbbell in each hand, the heaviest you can hold with good posture.
        2. Stand tall, shoulders down and back, stomach braced.
        3. Walk with short, steady steps, looking ahead.
        4. Set the weights down with a flat back before your grip gives out. With one dumbbell, carry it in one hand, stay upright, and switch hands each round.
        Feel it here
        • Grip and forearms.
        • Upper back and shoulders.
        • Core, keeping you upright.
        Not this
        • Leaning to one side or rounding forward: go lighter.
        • Shrugging: keep your shoulders down away from your ears.
        • Dropping the weights: put them down while you still control them.
        Scapular pull-uppull5 to 10 reps
        How
        1. Hang from a bar with straight arms, hands about shoulder-width apart.
        2. Without bending your elbows, pull your shoulders down away from your ears and slightly together.
        3. Your body rises an inch or two. Pause there.
        4. Let your shoulders rise back up slowly.
        Feel it here
        • Under the armpits.
        • Below the shoulder blades.
        Not this
        • Elbows bending: that is a pull-up. Keep the arms straight.
        • Kicking or swinging: start still, toes lightly on a box if you need to.
        • Shoulder pain at the bottom: do not fully relax there. Stay lightly engaged.
        Pogo hopsjump10 to 20 hops
        How
        1. Stand tall, feet hip-width apart, knees almost straight.
        2. Hop straight up and down using your ankles, staying on the balls of your feet.
        3. Keep each contact short, as if the floor were hot.
        4. Start with small hops. Go higher only once they are quiet and even.
        Feel it here
        • Calves and the back of the ankle, springy.
        Not this
        • Heels slamming down: make the hops smaller.
        • Knees bending a lot: that is a squat jump. Keep the bounce in the ankles.
        • Pain in the Achilles or the shins: stop for today, then try fewer, smaller hops in a few days.
        Squat jumpjump3 to 6 jumps, resetting between each
        How
        1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart.
        2. Dip into a quarter squat as your arms swing back.
        3. Swing your arms up and jump as high as you can.
        4. Land softly on the balls of your feet, hips and knees bending together. Stand still before the next one.
        Feel it here
        • Thighs and glutes driving.
        • Calves finishing the push.
        Not this
        • Loud, stiff landings: practise soft landings until they are quiet.
        • Knees caving in on the landing: jump smaller.
        • Jumping tired: every jump should be your best. Stop when they drop off.
